# Station register cross-check for the five located museum lots

The museum packet at commit `3a7e1e8` was frozen before this second read of Tydeman's register. The entries below can be checked visually in the original 1902 volume.

| Register station | Original date, position and site | Comparison with primary museum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | March 7, 1899. 7°27.5′ S, 113°08.5′ E, 37 m. Grey mud with small broken shells; 1,400 m from the Zwaantjes-droogte reef, Madura Strait. | Both historically and currently labeled names appear on the photographed shell lot, with station 1, 37 m and Madura Strait. The original longitude is explicitly east. [Register p3](https://archimer.ifremer.fr/doc/00000/4745/4237.pdf#page=163) · [ZMA.MOLL.31943](labels/ZMA.MOLL.31943.jpg) |
| 2 | March 8, 1899. 7°25′ S, 113°16′ E, 56 m. Grey mud with some radiolaria; Madura Strait. | The museum's old ticket, modern taxon label, date and depth all support the association. [Register p3](https://archimer.ifremer.fr/doc/00000/4745/4237.pdf#page=163) · [ZMA.MOLL.31942](labels/ZMA.MOLL.31942.jpg) |
| 60 | April 27–28, 1899. Blank latitude and longitude, 23 m. Lithothamnion in 3 m and less, reef; Haingsisi, Samau Island, Timor. | **Hold the specimen depth.** The handwritten museum ticket gives 36 m, echoed in the digital lot. The expedition station and locality agree, but the register gives 23 m and the printed species entry specifies habitat alone. The nominal station depth and individual collecting depth can describe different measurements. Their difference requires the field ledger or original accession documentation. [Register p5](https://archimer.ifremer.fr/doc/00000/4745/4237.pdf#page=165) · [Old lot ticket](labels/RMNH.MOL.193059_1.jpg) · [Schepman p111](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/2205140) |
| 164 | August 20, 1899. 1°42.5′ S, 130°47.5′ E, 32 m, dredge. Sand, small stones and shells. | Schepman's *gracilis* and *pfeifferianus* sections both cite this station with the same position, depth and substrate. The museum labels provide station 164 and Halmahera. The taxonomic disagreement and unresolved specimen counts persist. [Register p9](https://archimer.ifremer.fr/doc/00000/4745/4237.pdf#page=169) · [Two historical designations](labels/RMNH.MOL.193497.jpg) · [Schepman p112](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/2205139) |
| 234 | November 19–20, 1899. The surviving station-234 association is strong, though the book enumerates one shell.
