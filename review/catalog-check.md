# Catalog check for Pola stations 38 and 41

An independent agent checked GBIF on 25 November 2026. Its occurrence search returned eleven entries for **Pola** on the collection day. Nine molluscs were from station 37, and two plants were from Istria. The station 37 records share coordinates and date with station 38, while the report specifies different operations. One example is the *Ledella* record. GBIF lists HCMR as the publisher of these mollusc records. This is the same MedOBIS digitization used by the pilot. [Dated text query](https://api.gbif.org/v1/occurrence/search?q=Pola&eventDate=1890-09-06&limit=30) · [GBIF record](https://www.gbif.org/occurrence/4875618325) · [Dataset metadata](https://api.gbif.org/v1/dataset/3be44a65-238e-4412-80ad-3e47c2775b85) · [Printed station table](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/32372996)

The GBIF genus key for *Pterotrachea* returned 1,025 records without date or geographic restrictions. It returned zero worldwide for 1889–1891 and zero in the rectangle 32.0–33.1° N, 19.0–20.5° E across all years. These searches are limited to indexed taxonomies and metadata. The IDs and queries below allow fresh counts. [Genus control](https://api.gbif.org/v1/occurrence/search?taxonKey=5192663&limit=0) · [Historical dates](https://api.gbif.org/v1/occurrence/search?taxonKey=5192663&year=1889,1891&limit=0) · [Station area](https://api.gbif.org/v1/occurrence/search?taxonKey=5192663&decimalLatitude=32.0,33.1&decimalLongitude=19.0,20.5&limit=0)

Natural History Museum Vienna documents accession of Oberwimmer material in 1952/53 and material from the Pola expeditions. A register search under the printed names, stations and surface-net method could connect the printed event to cataloged material. The digitized search leaves specimen fate unknown.
